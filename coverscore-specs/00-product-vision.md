# CoverScore Product Vision

## Vision Statement

A world where every individual, family, and business in Africa understands their insurance risk, knows exactly what to do about it, and can act on it in minutes — not weeks.

## Mission

CoverScore makes insurance intelligence accessible to everyone by combining behavioural science, AI-driven risk assessment, and a high-touch advisor model on WhatsApp — Africa's most popular communication channel.

## Core Beliefs

1. **Insurance is protection, not a product.** People don't want insurance; they want to sleep soundly at night knowing their family, business, or health is protected.

2. **Most people underestimate their risk.** Behavioural economics shows that humans are optimistic about low-probability, high-impact events. CoverScore bridges this gap with personalised risk evidence.

3. **Risk is personal, not generic.** A 50-bed hospital in Lagos faces fundamentally different risks than a 20-bed clinic in Port Harcourt. Generic assessments produce generic recommendations that nobody acts on.

4. **WhatsApp is the right channel.** In markets where email open rates are low and app install friction is high, WhatsApp delivers 98% open rates and conversational engagement that feels natural.

5. **AI enables scale; humans close trust.** The assessment is fully automated by AI. But the conversion from insight to action happens through a trained advisor who uses AI-powered intelligence to personalise every interaction.

6. **Outcomes, not features.** Every feature is measured by whether it moves a user from awareness to protection — not by how sophisticated it is.

## Strategic Objectives

| Objective | Metric | Target |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Assessment completion rate | % of users who finish assessment | >75% |
| Advisor conversion rate | % of completed assessments → policy issued | >25% |
| User satisfaction | NPS score | >60 |
| Time-to-proposal | Assessment start to proposal sent | <24 hours |
| Renewal rate | % of policies renewed | >80% |
| WhatsApp response rate | % of users who engage within 24h | >90% |

## Target Users

- **Business owners** (SME, Manufacturing, Construction, Transport) who need operational risk coverage
- **Institutions** (Schools, Churches, Hospitals) with complex liability, property, and people risks
- **Individuals & Families** seeking health, life, income, and retirement protection
- **Young Professionals** building their first financial safety net
- **Advisors & Brokers** who need AI-powered tools to serve more clients better
