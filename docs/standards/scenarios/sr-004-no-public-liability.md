## 1. Scenario Overview

A well-run school has operated for over a decade with **no public liability protection**. The proprietor's position is: "We've never been sued, so we don't need it." The advisor must not argue — the assessment and honest dialogue must carry the message. The key skill is reframing protection as resilience, not prediction of disaster.

**Theme:** Liability exposure, "never been sued".
**CoverScore:** 38/100 — High Risk.
**Highest Priority:** Liability & Regulatory.

## 4. Advisor Brief™ (12 sections)

### 4.1 Client
Mr. Tunde Bakare, Proprietor, Kingsgate International School.

### 4.2 Organisation
Established (12 years), 500–800 students, ~40 staff, full programme incl. transport and events.

### 4.3 CoverScore
38/100 — High Risk.

### 4.4 Risk Level
High Risk (20–39).

### 4.5 Highest Priority
Liability exposure — the school has never been sued, but it has never been protected.

### 4.6 Biggest Insight
Kingsgate's strongest asset is its 12-year unblemished record. But a record is not protection. The school's most significant gap is not its teaching — it is that the school carries, alone, the full financial weight of anything that happens on its campus. Protection is not prediction of disaster; it is preparation for possibility.

### 4.7 Conversation Objectives
- Respect the proprietor's record; never argue.
- Let the assessment speak for the gap.
- Agree Liability as the priority.
- Agree reduction steps, then a clearly separated liability transfer option.

### 4.8 Recommended Opening Conversation
> "Mr. Bakare, thank you. Kingsgate has a wonderful record — twelve years without a serious incident is genuinely impressive. My job is to help you understand where your resilience stands so that record continues for another twelve years. Can we walk through the picture together?"

### 4.9 Suggested Next Action
Documented visitor/incident procedures (reduction) then a separate conversation on public liability protection.

### 4.10 Qualification Summary
Business Funnel™ entry; qualified; assessment completed by the proprietor.

### 4.11 CRM Insight
No advisor relationship. Proprietor proudly repeats "we've never been sued".

### 4.12 Advisor Preparation Notes
- Never argue with the "never been sued" position — respect it and move on.
- Hidden facts: no incident register; no visitor sign-in for all areas; events held with no risk assessment; no contractor insurance check; parent-handbook lacks safety terms.

Client response — **confident, slightly dismissive**:

> "Look, we've been here twelve years and never been sued. I don't believe in paying for problems that don't exist."

> "And that record is a real achievement — I'm not here to argue with it. The assessment isn't predicting a problem; it's measuring how the school would stand if something ever happened. Let me show you the gap, and you decide whether it's worth closing."
