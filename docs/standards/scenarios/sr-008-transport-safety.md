## 1. Scenario Overview

A school runs its own transport for hundreds of students daily. The buses operate, but there is no driver training programme, no vehicle inspection schedule, and no journey risk assessment. The proprietor values the buses as a competitive advantage. The advisor must protect that advantage by professionalising transport safety — reduction first, transfer for fleet exposure separated.

**Theme:** Transport safety, fleet discipline.
**CoverScore:** 29/100 — High Risk.
**Highest Priority:** Transport Safety.
