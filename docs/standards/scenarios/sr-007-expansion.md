## 1. Scenario Overview

A successful school is opening a second campus. The proprietor is excited and focused on construction and enrolment, but has not planned for the risks that come with expansion: doubled exposure, key-person dependence, construction-phase liability, and continuity across two sites. The advisor must champion growth while insisting the new site is protected from day one.

**Theme:** Growth planning, continuity, construction-phase risk.
**CoverScore:** 36/100 — High Risk.
**Highest Priority:** Business Continuity / Construction-phase protection.
