## 5. Certification Procedure

1. Assessor selects **one scenario at random**.
2. Candidate receives Advisor Brief, Report, client profile, CRM notes.
3. 40–45 min simulated Risk Review.
4. 15 min CRM documentation task.
5. 10 min debrief (handoff + reflection).
6. Assessor completes the observation sheet + rubric (100-point).
7. Certification decision per thresholds; critical failures override.
