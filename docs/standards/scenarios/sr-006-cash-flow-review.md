## 1. Scenario Overview

A small school has genuine cash-flow constraints. The proprietor cannot afford everything and feels overwhelmed. The advisor's task is to prioritise honestly within the budget, choosing the single highest-value step, and separating reduction (often free) from transfer (budgeted). This tests prioritisation discipline under financial pressure.

**Theme:** Cash-flow-constrained prioritisation.
**CoverScore:** 30/100 — High Risk.
**Highest Priority:** Student Safety (highest impact within limited budget).

## 9. Opening Conversation (Level 1)

Client response — **tired, honest**:

> "I want the school to be safe, but I genuinely have no money for extras right now. I don't want to be sold something I can't afford."

**Gold standard advisor response:**
> "I hear that, and I'm not going to sell you anything. The first improvements on this list cost almost nothing — they're about how the school is run, not what it buys. Let's start there, and be honest about what waits until the budget allows."
