## 1. Scenario Overview

A mature school has run for 18 years without a claim and the proprietor genuinely believes protection is a waste of money. This is the highest-resistance scenario. The advisor must earn a hearing through respect and evidence, surface real gaps, and agree — at minimum — a small, honest first step. Success is measured by reframing protection as resilience, not by selling anything.

**Theme:** Deep scepticism; 18-year record.
**CoverScore:** 40/100 — Needs Improvement.
**Highest Priority:** Liability & Property exposure (unprotected but unproven).

## 9. Opening Conversation (Level 1)

Client response — **brusque**:

> "I don't need insurance. I've run this school for 18 years without it. If you're here to sell, you can leave now."

**Gold standard advisor response:**
> "I respect that record, and I'm not here to sell. I'm here because the school has a score it deserves to see. If you give me ten minutes to show you the picture, you'll decide for yourself what's worth doing. If nothing is, we shake hands and I leave."
