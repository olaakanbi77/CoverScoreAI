## 1. Scenario Overview

A long-established school has serious fire-protection gaps: no working fire alarm in part of the building, unserviced extinguishers, and no fire drill record. The proprietor is aware but always "getting to it". The advisor must surface the facts, frame fire safety as protection of children and continuity — not fear — and agree prioritised reduction plus a clearly separated property-transfer option.

**Theme:** Fire safety, regulatory readiness.
**CoverScore:** 21/100 — High Risk.
**Highest Priority:** Property Protection / Fire Safety.

## 17. Recommendation Challenge

**Gold standard:** clarify cleanly — protecting buildings financially (transfer) is different from making the building safe (reduction); both matter, done in order, never mixed.
