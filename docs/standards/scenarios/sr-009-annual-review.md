## 1. Scenario Overview

A returning client improved their CoverScore from 46 to 61 in a year. This is the annual review. The advisor must celebrate the improvement honestly, compare the two scores, re-prioritise the remaining gaps, and agree next year's plan. The critical skill: praise real progress without manufacturing new risk, and re-prioritise with the client's evidence.

**Theme:** Annual review, improvement, re-prioritisation.
**CoverScore:** 61/100 — Moderately Resilient (up from 46/Needs Improvement).
**Highest Priority:** Remaining largest gap (Business Continuity).

## 15. Recommendation Test

- Reduction: two-month continuity plan, backup responsibility, alternate-premises arrangement.
- Transfer: continuity protection only if a gap remains (separated, evidence-based).
- Further Investigation: only if the continuity risk needs quantification.
- Not Currently Appropriate: re-selling resolved risks — do not manufacture.

---

## 16. Risk Reduction vs Risk Transfer Exercise

**Reduction (first):** continuity plan, backup ownership, premises arrangement.
**Transfer (separated):** continuity protection, only where a real gap remains.
