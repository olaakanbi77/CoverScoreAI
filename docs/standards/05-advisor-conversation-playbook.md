# CoverScore Advisor Conversation Playbook™

**Version:** v2 | **Status:** Canonical | **Owner:** CoverScore Advisory Leadership

---

## 1. Purpose

The Conversation Playbook equips the Certified CoverScore Risk Advisor™ to have honest, resilient, professional conversations that protect what matters. It defines the CARE™ framework, the twelve conversations an advisor conducts, communication principles, and the exact language the platform and advisors use.

---

## 2. The CARE™ Framework

Every client conversation follows CARE:

| Letter | Stage | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **C** | **Connect** | Establish rapport and context — build trust, confirm purpose |
| **A** | **Ask** | Explore the client's concerns, priorities and experiences — ask, don't assume |
| **R** | **Reframe** | Connect the client's concerns to the risk intelligence |
| **E** | **Enable** | Help the client understand practical next steps and move toward action |

**Rules**
- Never skip a stage.
- Ask is driven by genuine curiosity, not script.
- Reframe connects the client's concerns to the risk intelligence; responses are always reduction-first.
- Enable ends with agreed next steps, dates, and ownership.

---

## 3. The Twelve Conversations

1. **The Intro Call** — qualify, set context, explain the process.
2. **The Assessment Invitation** — invite the client to complete the assessment.
3. **The Assessment Check-In** — support completion, answer questions.
4. **The Risk Review** — the core conversation (CARE).
5. **The Priority Conversation** — agree the Highest Priority together.
6. **The Reduction Conversation** — Risk Reduction actions.
7. **The Transfer Conversation** — Risk Transfer options.
8. **The Protection Strategy** — agree the plan and next steps.
9. **The Follow-Up** — check progress on commitments.
10. **The Annual Review** — re-assess, compare, plan.
11. **The Referral Conversation** — natural invitation to refer.
12. **The Concern Conversation** — handle any client concern with honesty.

---

## 4. Communication Principles

1. **Plain language** — no jargon, no complexity.
2. **Client-first** — strength before gap.
3. **Ask, don't assume** — genuine curiosity.
4. **Listen more than you speak.**
5. **Confirm understanding** before moving on.
6. **Never fear, never pressure, never mislead.**
7. **Every conversation ends with agreed next steps.**

## 6. Conversation Scripts (Guides, not scripts)

### 6.1 The Intro Call
> "Thank you for your time. My name is [Name], a Certified CoverScore Risk Advisor. I help [businesses/families] understand their resilience and protect what matters. This first conversation is simply to understand your situation and explain how we work. Is now a good time to talk?"

### 6.2 The Assessment Invitation
> "The best way to understand your current resilience is a short digital assessment. It takes about [X] minutes and produces a CoverScore out of 100, plus a clear picture of where you are strong and where improvement helps. After that, we meet to go through the results together."

### 6.3 The Risk Review — Connect
> "Thank you for making time. My role is to help you understand your resilience and agree what to prioritise. We'll go through the CoverScore, the strongest areas, and then the areas where improvement has the most value. There's no pressure — my job is to make the picture clear for you."

### 6.4 The Risk Review — Ask
> "Help me understand — what does [topic] look like for you at the moment? What's working well? What worries you, if anything?"

### 6.5 The Risk Review — Reframe
> "Based on what you've told me, the highest priority is [X]. That connects directly to what the assessment shows — [gap]. The first response is [Risk Reduction action]. Once that's in place, we can look at [Risk Transfer option] as a way to protect against the financial impact. I'll present these as two separate things so the picture stays clear."

### 6.6 The Risk Review — Enable
> "So the plan is: [Reduction action] by [date], [Transfer option] reviewed by [date], and I'll follow up on [date]. Does that work for you?"

## 7. Handling Objections (CARE-based)

| Objection | Response principle |
|-----------|--------------------|
| "We can't afford it." | Reframe: prioritise by value, not cost. Explore what is affordable. |
| "We've never had a problem." | Acknowledge; focus on resilience, not fear. |
| "Insurance is unnecessary." | Explore the true exposure; let the assessment speak. |
| "We're already covered." | Ask what's covered; the assessment reveals gaps. |
| "We need to think about it." | Agree; set a specific follow-up date. |
| "Insurance companies don't pay." | Acknowledge the experience; discuss clarity and documentation. |

---

## 8. Conversation Quality Standards

1. Client speaks at least half the time.
2. Questions outnumber statements.
3. Every stage of CARE is visibly present.
4. Risk Reduction presented before Risk Transfer.
5. No fear, pressure, or jargon.
6. Next steps agreed and clear.

## 9. The Conversation Promise
