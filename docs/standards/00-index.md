## Reading order

1. Start with **01 Architecture** to understand the platform.
2. Read **02 Intelligence Standards** before touching any score, pillar, report, or Advisor Brief.
3. Consult **03 Assessment Catalogue** for product names, IDs, and pillar sets.
4. Use **04 Advisor Playbook** + **05 Conversation Playbook** for any advisor-facing content.
5. Apply **06 Simulation Framework** for any assessment, certification, or roleplay content.
6. Reference **07 Scenario Library** for practical exercises, certification examinations, and AI roleplay.

## Relationship to training packages

- **CCA 105 — Practical Risk Advisory & Client Assessment:** practical examination manual = Scenario Library (assessor selects one scenario at random). Assessment = 100-point Simulation Framework.
- **CCA 106 — Risk Reviews:** scenario library used for advanced risk review practice.
- **CCA 107 — Portfolio Management:** scenarios support multi-client judgement work.
- **CCA 108 — Integrated Professional Practice:** scenarios support the final integrated advisory simulation.

## Canonical facts (do not diverge)

- One CoverScore™ per assessment: **0–100**.
- Score bands: **80–100 Highly Resilient · 60–79 Moderately Resilient · 40–59 Needs Improvement · 20–39 High Risk · 0–19 Critical Risk**.
- 5–9 Risk Pillars™ per assessment.
- Risk Reduction™ and Risk Transfer™ are **never mixed**; Risk Reduction is always presented first.
- Advisor responsibility begins at the **Advisor Brief™**; the platform automates prospecting, qualification, and assessment.
- "CoverScore does not sell insurance; CoverScore builds resilience."
