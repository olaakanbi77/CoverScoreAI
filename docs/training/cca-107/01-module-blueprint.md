# CCA 107 — Part 1: Module Blueprint

**Module Title:** CCA 107 — Professional Risk Advisory Practice & Client Portfolio Management
**Package Production Standard:** Complete Training Package v2 (Parts 1–6)

---

## 1. Module Overview

CCA 107 develops the Advisor's ability to manage a client's risk portfolio across time.
The Advisor has learned how to interpret intelligence (CCA 105) and turn it into trusted
conversations and growth (CCA 106). Now the Advisor must keep creating value after the
first policy: monitoring portfolios, tracking continuous risk intelligence, running
annual reassessments and professional Risk Reviews™, advising after claims and incidents,
keeping professional records, and measuring value honestly.

**The module's core principle:**

> **A policy is a transaction. A portfolio is a relationship.**

The module therefore answers one central question the Advisor must carry into every
portfolio:

> **How do I continuously create value for this client?**

## 2. Module Purpose

Positioned between CCA 106 (use the intelligence) and CCA 108 (demonstrate the complete
advisory cycle), CCA 107 teaches portfolio stewardship: building and managing client risk
portfolios, monitoring risk change signals, conducting annual resiliency reviews and
professional Risk Reviews™, delivering post-loss advisory, maintaining documentation and
CRM discipline, and measuring client value and advisor performance.

## 4. Learning Outcomes

By the end of CCA 107, the learner will be able to:

1. Manage a client risk portfolio across multiple exposures and across time.
2. Monitor the portfolio using continuous risk intelligence and risk change signals.
3. Conduct annual reassessments and client resilience reviews.
4. Run a professional CoverScore Risk Review™ from preparation to next action.
5. Advise on claims, incidents, and post-loss risk.
6. Document advisory activity and maintain professional CRM records.
7. Measure client value and advisor performance honestly.
8. Conduct a complete portfolio-management review.

## 5. Competencies Developed

### Technical
- Portfolio composition and coverage reconciliation
- Continuous risk intelligence and monitoring cadence
- Annual reassessment and resilience review design
- Professional Risk Review™ delivery (seven stages)
- Post-loss advisory and the six-stage recovery framework

### Professional
- Claims and incident response discipline
- Documentation and the advisory record
- CRM discipline (Who? What? Why? When? Decision? Next step?)
- Client Value Score and portfolio metrics
- Assessment of advisor performance beyond premium

### Behavioural
- Relationship-over-transaction thinking (the portfolio principle)
- Honest prioritisation — reduction before transfer
- Client-first response after incidents (blame-free)
- Professional judgment under new information
- Long-term value measurement

## 9. Assessment Strategy

**Instrument:** One Portfolio Management Simulation, scored on 8 parts, 100 points:
Portfolio Management 15, Continuous Risk Intelligence 10, Resilience Reviews 15,
Professional Risk Review 15, Claims & Post-Loss Advisory 10, CRM & Documentation 10,
Client Value 10, Portfolio Simulation 15.

**Pass standard:** 80–100 Competent · 70–79 Conditional Pass · 60–69 Remediation
Required · below 60 Not Yet Competent. Any critical failure (renewal-only review, ignored
campus/buses/students/staff, ignored student accident, ignored unresolved transport risk,
products before exposure, failure to document, unsupported coverage assurances, no agreed
follow-up) means not competent until coaching is completed.

**Scenario:** Flourish College in its review-cycle state (initial CoverScore 41, current
CoverScore 58), with the one-year-later facts revealed by the learner's questions.

## 11. Certification Standard

To pass, learners must demonstrate competence through the Portfolio Management Simulation
across eight parts at a total of **80 / 100 or above**, with no critical failure
condition triggered. Certification is logged in the Academy with the per-part score
breakdown retained for audit.
