# CCA 107 — Lesson 5: Claims, Incidents & Post-Loss Risk Advisory

**Module:** CCA 107 — Professional Risk Advisory Practice & Client Portfolio Management
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~25 minutes | **Independent:** ~15 minutes

---

## 1. Lesson Overview

This lesson teaches the advisor to remain valuable when something goes wrong. Learners
see the incident call as one of the most important moments in the advisor-client
relationship, practise the advisory response that stabilises first and then reviews, run
the post-loss framework from stabilisation to protection, and turn a loss into new risk
intelligence rather than a closed claim.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Recognise the incident moment as a defining relationship moment.
2. Respond to an incident with stabilisation first, then structured review.
3. Run the post-loss framework: Stabilise → Document → Understand → Learn → Improve → Protect.
4. Use the post-loss review questions to understand cause and recurrence risk.
5. Convert a loss into risk intelligence and a learning loop.

## 5. Learner Handbook

### 5.1 The incident moment

The incident call ("We've just had a serious accident at the school") is one of the most
important moments in the advisor-client relationship. How the advisor shows up here
decides how the client feels about the relationship for years.

### 5.2 Beyond claims administration

"Okay. Send the claim documents." is administratively correct and relationally thin.
Claims processing matters, but the professional's responsibility goes further: stabilise
the situation, then review what happened, what protection applies, and what lessons
should be captured.

### 5.3 The advisory response

Acknowledge, stabilise, then review: "First, let's make sure the immediate situation is
being handled appropriately. Once it's under control, we'll review what happened, what
protection applies, and whether there are lessons we should capture to reduce the chance
of recurrence."

### 5.4 The post-loss framework

**Stabilise** (immediate) → **Document** (claim) → **Understand** (review) → **Learn**
(identify cause) → **Improve** (strengthen controls) → **Protect** (review financial
protection).

### 5.5 The post-loss review

Ask: What happened? What caused it? What controls existed? Which controls failed? Was it
previously identified? Was there a near miss? What financial impact occurred? What
protection responded? What should change?

### 5.6 A loss is intelligence

A major loss can move the CoverScore (63 → 51). A loss should not simply become a closed
claim — it should become new risk intelligence that drives the learning loop: Incident →
Claim → Intelligence → Risk Improvement → Reassessment → Better Protection.

### 5.7 The blame-free conversation

Frame the review as learning, not blame: "The purpose of this review isn't to assign
blame. It's to understand what happened and determine what we can change so the same
weakness doesn't create another loss."

## 6. Practical Exercises

**Exercise 5.1 — The first response (15 min)**
Write your response to an incident call using the Scene 3 pattern. No claim-form-only line.

**Exercise 5.2 — Post-loss framework (20 min)**

at each?

**Exercise 5.3 — Nine-question review (15 min)**
Run a post-loss review on a practice incident using the nine questions. Note which
answers would change the protection conversation.
