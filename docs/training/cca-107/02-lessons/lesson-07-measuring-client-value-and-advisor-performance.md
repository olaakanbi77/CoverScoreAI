# CCA 107 — Lesson 7: Measuring Client Value & Advisor Performance

**Module:** CCA 107 — Professional Risk Advisory Practice & Client Portfolio Management
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~25 minutes | **Independent:** ~15 minutes

---

## 1. Lesson Overview

This lesson teaches the advisor to measure success beyond premium volume. Learners
replace the old scorecard (premium, policies, new clients, renewals) with a broader
view of client value — risk improvement, protection alignment, response quality,
relationship strength, retention, and growth — and learn the Client Value Score and the
portfolio metrics that make professional performance visible.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Explain why premium volume alone cannot measure advisory performance.
2. Define the six dimensions of client value.
3. Monitor the ten portfolio metrics.
4. Use the Client Value Score weights: Risk Improvement 30%, Relationship Health 20%, Retention 20%, Engagement 15%, Growth 15%.
5. Judge the "How much value did I create?" question alongside "How much did I sell?"

## 5. Learner Handbook

### 5.1 The old scorecard is not enough

Premium, policies, new clients, and renewals are necessary numbers — but they do not
tell the whole story of advisory performance.

### 5.2 The six dimensions of client value

- **Risk Improvement** — is the client becoming more resilient?
- **Protection Alignment** — does current protection match the current risk profile?
- **Response Quality** — how well does the advisor respond when it matters?
- **Relationship Strength** — how durable and trusted is the relationship?
- **Retention** — does the client stay across cycles?
- **Growth** — does the relationship expand over time?

### 5.3 The ten portfolio metrics

Active clients; retention rate; assessment completion; review completion; risk
improvement; unresolved high-priority gaps; cross-solution relevance; referral
generation; client engagement; portfolio growth.

### 5.4 Why premium alone fools you

Advisor A: 100 clients, ₦50m premium, low engagement, few reviews, high unresolved gaps.
Advisor B: 80 clients, ₦42m premium, high review completion, strong retention, improving
resilience, high engagement. **The stronger advisory practice cannot be determined from
premium alone.**

### 5.5 The Client Value Score

A balanced view of client value:

- Risk Improvement **30%**
- Relationship Health **20%**
- Retention **20%**
- Engagement **15%**
- Growth **15%**

The objective is not to replace commercial metrics — it is to place commercial
performance inside a broader professional context.

### 5.6 The great advisor's question

Ask "How much did I sell?" and "How much value did I create?" The strongest advisor
creates measurable client value while building sustainable commercial performance.

## 7. Case Study

**Scenario:** Two advisors present to the Academy. Advisor X has 100 clients and ₦50m
premium, few reviews, low engagement, and several unresolved high-priority gaps. Advisor
Y has 80 clients and ₦42m premium, all reviews completed, high retention, improving
resilience, and high engagement.

**Tasks:**
1. State why premium alone cannot decide the stronger practice.
2. Score both advisors across the five Client Value components.
3. List which portfolio metrics would settle the question.
4. Write the professional-development recommendation for each advisor.
