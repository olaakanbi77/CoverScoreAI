# CCA 107 — Student Workbook (Part 3)

**Module:** CCA 107 — Professional Risk Advisory Practice & Client Portfolio Management
**Use:** Complete alongside the 8 lessons; submit the marked sections for their lesson's
completion standard and the module assessment.

---

## How to use this workbook

For each lesson, complete the sections after watching the video. Store them as your
personal practice bank — they become your real-world templates the day you manage a
portfolio.

## Lesson 1 — Managing Client Risk Portfolios

1. Write the portfolio principle from memory and explain the difference between a policy
   and a portfolio.
2. Build a portfolio snapshot for Flourish College: exposures, current protection, and
   the gaps you can already name.
3. Reconcile one of your own clients (or the module case) across the four responses:
   Reduce / Transfer / Accept / Avoid.

## Lesson 2 — Portfolio Monitoring & Continuous Risk Intelligence

1. Draw the continuous-intelligence flow: Client → new information → CoverScore
   Intelligence → Risk Change Signal → Advisor Review → Client Engagement.
2. Assign a monitoring cadence (high-priority frequent · standard periodic · stable
   scheduled) to five risks in the module case.
3. For three pieces of new information, write the risk change signal and the advisor
   action it triggers.

## Lesson 3 — Annual Reassessments & Client Resilience Reviews

1. Write the purpose of the annual reassessment in one paragraph.
2. For Florence James (41 → 58), list the progress to acknowledge and the gaps to raise.
3. Write the opening line of a resilience review that acknowledges the client's work
   before discussing gaps.

## Lesson 4 — Conducting Professional CoverScore Risk Reviews™

1. Write the seven stages of the Risk Review™ from memory.
2. Build a complete Risk Review™ sequence (Prepare → Open → Discover → Interpret →
   Advise → Document → Next Action) for the module case.
3. Write the line that reconciles the second campus and extra buses with the existing
   portfolio.

## Lesson 5 — Claims, Incidents & Post-Loss Risk Advisory

1. Write the six-stage post-loss framework: Stabilise → Document → Understand → Learn →
   Improve → Protect.
2. Write the nine post-loss questions you would ask after an incident.
3. Draft your opening line for the incident call that is calm, human, and blame-free.

## Lesson 6 — Client Documentation, CRM & Advisory Records

1. List what documentation protects (Client, Advisor, Organisation, Decision Quality,
   Continuity).
2. Rewrite the BAD note ("Spoke to client. Interested.") into a GOOD note for the
   expansion scenario.
3. Build a five-step advisory record for a recent review.

## Lesson 7 — Measuring Client Value & Advisor Performance

1. Write the six dimensions of client value.
2. Score a client on the Client Value Score (Risk Improvement 30 · Relationship Health 20
   · Retention 20 · Engagement 15 · Growth 15) with evidence for each.
3. Answer both questions for yourself: "How much did I sell?" and "How much value did I
   create?"

## Lesson 8 — Portfolio Management Simulation

1. Prepare the portfolio review: one-page plan across the seven stages from the
   intelligence pack (brief, 41 → 58 history, CRM record).
2. Reconcile each piece of new information (campus, buses, students, staff, accident,
   controls, procedures) against the current portfolio.
3. Record a full 15-minute practice simulation and self-assess on the 9-category rubric.
