# CCA 107 — Practical Tools (Part 3)

**Module:** CCA 107 — Professional Risk Advisory Practice & Client Portfolio Management

Practical, reusable tools for the working advisor and the training room.

## Tool 1 — The Portfolio Principle Card

Keep on the desk during any client call:

> **A POLICY IS A TRANSACTION.**
>
> **A PORTFOLIO IS A RELATIONSHIP.**

The measure of mastery: *How do I continuously create value for this client?*

## Tool 2 — The Continuous-Intelligence Flow Card

> Client → new information → CoverScore Intelligence → Risk Change Signal → Advisor
> Review → Client Engagement

The advisor is never passive between reviews.

## Tool 3 — The Monitoring Cadence Card

- **High-priority risk** → frequent monitoring.
- **Standard risk** → periodic monitoring.
- **Stable risk** → scheduled monitoring.

## Tool 4 — The Four-Response Model

Every exposure is managed by one of four responses:

- **Reduce** — lower the likelihood or impact.
- **Transfer** — move the financial consequence to appropriate protection.
- **Accept** — retain a consciously chosen exposure.
- **Avoid** — remove the exposure where possible.

Risk Reduction™ first, Risk Transfer™ second — never mixed.

## Tool 5 — The Risk Review™ Card (seven stages)

Prepare → Open → Discover → Interpret → Advise → Document → Next Action.

Key reconciliation line: *"The portfolio was built for the client as they were last
year. Let's review what has changed so the protection still reflects the client."*

## Tool 6 — The Post-Loss Framework Card

**Stabilise → Document → Understand → Learn → Improve → Protect**

Response rules: calm first, human first, blame-free. Never lead an incident response
with a product.

## Tool 7 — The Documentation Standard Card

> If an important advisory decision is not documented, the organisation may later have no
> reliable record of what was understood, discussed or agreed.

The advisory record: **knew → discovered → advised → decided → what happens next.**

## Tool 8 — The CRM Discipline Card

Every meaningful engagement answers:

> **Who? What? Why? When? Decision? Next step?**

## Tool 9 — The Client Value Score Card

- Risk Improvement **30%**
- Relationship Health **20%**
- Retention **20%**
- Engagement **15%**
- Growth **15%**

Ask both questions: *How much did I sell?* AND *How much value did I create?*

## Tool 10 — The Critical-Failure Poster

Post in the training room. The simulation/assessment fails (until coaching) if the
Advisor: treats the review as renewal-only · ignores the new campus/buses/students/staff ·
ignores the student accident · ignores the unresolved transport risk · raises products
before understanding exposure · fails to document · makes unsupported coverage assurances ·
fails to agree follow-up actions.
