# CCA 105 — Part 4: Assessment Pack

**Module:** CCA 105 — Practical Risk Advisory & Client Assessment
**Pass mark:** 80% | **Time:** ~2 hours

Comprises five assessment modes: Practical, Written, AI, Instructor, and Certification.

---

## 1. Assessment Overview

| Mode | Marks | Weight | Duration |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| A. Practical assessment (simulated Risk Review) | 50 | 40% | 45 min |
| B. Written assessment (advisory summary) | 30 | 25% | 45 min |
| C. AI assessment (AI-graded scenario set) | 20 | 15% | 20 min |
| D. Instructor assessment (professional conduct) | 20 | 20% | observed during A–B |
| **Total** | **120** | **100%** | **~2h10m** |

Final score = (A+B+C+D)/120 × 100. **Pass = 80 or above.**

## 2. Assessment Scenario (shared across A and B)

> **Canonical source:** the School Resilience™ Scenario Library
> (`docs/standards/scenarios/`) is the official practical workbook for CCA 105. In
> certification the assessor selects one scenario (SR-001–SR-010) at random. For the
> in-module assessment below, the standard scenario is used as the exemplar; assessors
> may substitute any library scenario at the same difficulty.

Learners receive exactly what an advisor receives in real life:

1. **AI-generated Advisor Brief™** — for **GreenField Agro-Processing Ltd** (CoverScore 54,
   Needs Attention; business interruption, key-person, personal guarantee NGN 350M,
   succession, cyber).
2. **CoverScore Report™** — full report including pillar scores, fingerprint, top risks,
   exposure, recommendations.
3. **Client profile** — Mr. Chidi Okonkwo (54), founder; finance manager will attend.
4. **CRM notes** — assessment timing, confidence (medium), chat transcript highlights.

The learner must:
1. **Analyse** the assessment (facts, confidence, matrix, top-3 priorities).
2. **Prepare** for the meeting (6-step prep pack).
3. **Conduct** a simulated Risk Review.
4. **Produce** a written advisory summary.
5. **Recommend** next steps.

## 3. A. Practical Assessment (50 marks)

**Format:** 30-minute live simulated Risk Review with the assessor playing Mr. Okonkwo,
followed by a 10-minute Q&A.

| Criteria | Marks | Evidence expected |
|----------|-------|-------------------|
| Assessment interpretation | 10 | Correctly reads report, bands, confidence, matrix |
| Report explanation | 10 | Explains score/pillars/gap in plain language |
| Discovery questioning | 10 | Confirms facts, builds ≥1 risk chain, funnel in use |
| Professional judgement | 10 | Defends priority order with the client's own numbers |
| Client engagement & trust | 10 | Empathy, palette control, permission-based, no pressure |
| **Total** | **50** | |

Level descriptors per criterion: 0–2 (missed), 3–4 (partial), 5–6 (adequate), 7–8
(good), 9–10 (exceptional).

---

## 4. B. Written Assessment (30 marks)

**Format:** produce the advisory summary + action plan (paper or digital) based on the
same scenario, within 45 minutes.

| Section | Marks | Requirements |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| Executive summary | 5 | Score, band, story in ≤150 words |
| Priority risk analysis | 8 | Top-3 risks with impact estimates and traceable reasoning |
| Recommendation plan | 8 | Sequenced, affordable options; links back to assessment |
| Client action plan + next steps | 5 | Decision, next step, date, follow-up rhythm |
| Professional language | 4 | Plain language, no jargon, no fear-based framing |
| **Total** | **30** | |

## 5. C. AI Assessment (20 marks)

**Format:** the AI tutor/assessor grades the learner's scenario answers and knowledge
checks. Uses the AI grading prompts in `05-ai-companion`.

| Component | Marks |
|-----------|-------|
| Scenario multiple-choice set (10 × 1) | 10 |
| Short-answer triage (3 × 2) | 6 |
| 90-second interpretation transcript | 4 |
| **Total** | **20** |

Topics drawn from: process steps, score formula consequences, facts-vs-suggestions,
translation, confidence handling, review preparation, empathy ladder.

## 8. Certification Standard

To pass, the learner must demonstrate competence (80%+) in **all seven competency
areas**:

1. Assessment interpretation
2. Report explanation
3. Discovery questioning
4. Communication
5. Professional judgement
6. Risk prioritisation
7. Client engagement

Any single area scoring below 50% on the rubric requires targeted re-assessment —
regardless of the total. Certification is issued only once the 80% overall
**and** the no-below-50% rule are both met.
