# CCA 105 — Instructor Guide (Part 3)

**Module:** CCA 105 — Practical Risk Advisory & Client Assessment
**Use:** Deliver virtual/classroom workshops, simulations, and debriefs.

---

## 1. Your Role

You are teaching advisors to move from AI insight to human advisory. Your chief value is
enabling safe practice: learners should make mistakes in simulation, not in front of
clients. Create a psychologically safe room where practice is frequent and feedback is
specific.

## 2. Session Plan (workshops)

| Session | Duration | Led by | Purpose |
|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| Lesson 2 workshop | 20–30 min | Instructor | Confidence triage on sample assessments |
| Lesson 3 workshop | 25 min | Instructor | Brief annotation + personalisation |
| Lesson 4 workshop | 25 min | Instructor | Report translation + hard-message practice |
| Lesson 5 workshop | 25 min | Instructor | Prep-pack build + opening rehearsal |
| Lesson 6 workshop | 30 min | Instructor | Triad discovery drills |
| Lesson 7 workshop | 45 min | Instructor | Cross-industry interpretation + presentations |

Total workshop time ≈ 3 hours (matches module blueprint).

## 3. Room Setup

- **Breakout triads** for discovery listening: advisor / client / observer.
- **Role-play partners** for the lesson-8 simulation and the module assessment.
- **Shared artefacts** on screen: Advisor Brief, CoverScore Report, sample assessmments,
  templates, and the CRM views.
- **Timekeeping:** keep the review simulations strictly timed (30 min for the capstone).

## 4. Facilitation Script (50-minute debrief template)

| Time | Segment | Suggested words |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| 5 min | Celebrate | "What felt hard today? What felt surprisingly natural?" |
| 10 min | Reconnect to the process | "Which of the 8 steps did we practise most? Why does that step matter?" |
| 15 min | Live demonstration | Model a 3-minute micro-review on a fresh record; narrate your thinking aloud |
| 10 min | Critique practice | Learners repeat with a new record; coach on two behaviours max |
| 10 min | Bridge to assessment | "The module assessment is the same loop, longer and scored. Book your slot." |

## 5. Common Learner Errors and Coaching

| Error | What to correct | Coaching intervention |
|-------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Reading the Brief aloud | Adapts a scaffold into a script | "Paraphrase it. Own it. Say it your way." |
| Ignoring confidence | Treats every score as gospel | "Show me the warning signs. What's still unverified?" |
| Pitching too early | Jumps to options before impact | "Build the risk chain first — let them feel the impact." |
| Interrogating | Multi-part, closed questioning | "One question. Then silence. Then reflect." |
| Fear-based framing | Exaggerates threats | "Name it plainly, normalise, reframe as controllable." |
| No close | Ends without action plan | "What is the decision? What is the next step? When?" |

## 6. Assessment Logistics

- Brief learners on the module assessment format in Lesson 8 (see
  `04-assessment/assessment-pack.md`).
- Book one-on-one simulated reviews; use the instructor scoring rubric.
- Provide written feedback within 48 hours: one strength, one area to grow, one
  recommended next practice.

## 7. Materials Checklist

Per learner: Student Workbook, worksheets pack, templates pack, conversation guides,
sample assessment pack (3+ records), CRM access, and a copy of their own Module
Assessment brief.
