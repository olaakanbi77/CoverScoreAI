## Worksheet 5 — Client Question Bank

**Purpose:** Source of ready questions for discovery and risk reviews. Tick the ones you
use per client.

**Openers (rapport + purpose)**
- "How have things changed since you first started this?"
- "What prompted you to complete the assessment — anything specific?"
- "What does a good year look like for you?"

**Business / context**
- "Tell me about the people your business depends on most."
- "What keeps you up at night about cash flow?"
- "What happens if you or a key partner cannot work for three months?"

**Verification (confirm assessment facts)**
- "Just to be sure, your assessment says no business interruption cover — correct?"
- "Has anything changed since you filled in the assessment?"
- "What exactly does your current building cover include?"

**Impact (risk chains)**
- "If that happened, what would it cost the business directly?"
- "What else would feel it? Staff? Clients? Your loans?"
- "How long could you keep paying fixed costs without revenue?"

**Priority and money**
- "If you could fix one thing today, which would it be?"
- "How do you tend to handle new spending — monthly or one-off?"
- "What's your comfort level on protecting the biggest items first?"

**Close / handoff**
- "Which of these next steps makes most sense to you?"
- "If we do this one thing first, what would that unblock?"
- "Can we set a date to review progress on this plan?"
