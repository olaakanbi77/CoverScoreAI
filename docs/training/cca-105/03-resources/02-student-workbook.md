# CCA 105 — Student Workbook (Part 3)

**Module:** CCA 105 — Practical Risk Advisory & Client Assessment
**Use:** Complete alongside the 8 lessons; submit the marked sections for their lesson's
completion standard and the module assessment.

---

## How to use this workbook

For each lesson, complete the sections after watching the video. Store them as your
personal practice bank — they become your real-world templates the day you talk to a
client.

## Lesson 1 — The Modern CoverScore Advisory Process

1. Draw the 10-step workflow and label each step as System (S), Advisor (A), or Both (B).
2. Write a 60-second explanation using "AI computes, advisor advises."
3. List 3 things in your current routine that AI could automate, and 3 that must stay human.

## Lesson 2 — Understanding CoverScore Assessments

1. In your own words: "Score = 100 × (safe weight / total weight)". Why do skipped
   questions distort the score upward?
2. Write the five bands from memory. Self-check against Lesson 2.
3. For a record completed in under 3 minutes, write the warning signs and the
   verification questions you would ask.

## Lesson 3 — Understanding the Advisor Brief™

1. Label a sample Brief: F (fact) or S (suggestion) for every line.
2. Rewrite a generic Copilot opening using "their words, their context, their strengths."
3. Run the 5-point Brief QA checklist on your own sample.

## Lesson 4 — Interpreting CoverScore Reports™

1. Translate five engine phrases into client language.
2. Write your personal version of the empathy ladder for low scores.
3. Convert a sample report's top gaps into a 3-item review agenda.

## Lesson 5 — Preparing for a Risk Review

1. Build a complete 6-step prep pack for the SR-001 Flourish College client (Lesson 8
   case) or a client you know.
2. Record your personalised opening; write what you changed after listening back.
3. Define your "one decision" for the meeting — and 3 winning outcomes that are not sales.

## Lesson 6 — Professional Discovery Conversations

1. Write the full 6-stage arc for a client of your choice.
2. Build a six-question funnel (2 broad, 2 probing, 1 reflective, 1 consequence).
3. Log three phrases you heard yourself use that worked — and one you will never use again.

## Lesson 7 — Assessment Interpretation Workshop

1. Interpret three different-domain records (fingerprint, matrix, top-3, presentation).
2. Classify each as silver-bullet or systemic, with evidence.
3. Deliver a 90-second presentation to a partner; note their feedback.

## Lesson 8 — Assessment Review & Client Handoff Simulation

1. Complete all four phases for the assigned client.
2. Self-assess against the eight assessment parts (score / /each).
3. Write your single biggest improvement goal for the module assessment.

## Optional extension (module assessment prep)

Re-run Lesson 8's SR-001 Flourish College simulation against the official scoring
rubric in `04-assessment/assessment-pack.md` under exam conditions the day before your
assessment.
