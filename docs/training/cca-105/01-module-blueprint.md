# CCA 105 — Part 1: Module Blueprint

**Module Title:** CCA 105 — Practical Risk Advisory & Client Assessment
**Package Production Standard:** Complete Training Package v2 (Parts 1–6)

---

## 1. Module Overview

This module bridges the gap between automated AI assessments and professional human
advisory. Learners master how to:

- Understand AI-generated CoverScore Assessments.
- Read and interpret Advisor Briefs™.
- Explain CoverScore Reports™ professionally.
- Prepare for Risk Reviews.
- Conduct discovery conversations.
- Build trust before discussing protection strategies.
- Transition seamlessly from AI insights to human advisory.

By the end of the module, learners will be able to confidently conduct their first
structured CoverScore Risk Review.

## 2. Module Purpose

Learners enter this module having studied the CoverScore methodology (CCA 103) and the
risk advisor mindset (CCA 104). CCA 105 is the first "hands-on" module: it turns
knowledge into a repeatable, professional advisory engagement — from the moment an AI
assessment lands in their CRM to the moment they hand the client off to a
recommendations meeting.

## 4. Learning Outcomes

On successful completion, learners will be able to:

1. Explain the modern CoverScore advisory process.
2. Interpret AI-generated risk assessments accurately.
3. Read and use the Advisor Brief™ effectively.
4. Interpret every section of a CoverScore Report™.
5. Prepare professionally for a Risk Review.
6. Conduct effective discovery conversations.
7. Analyse assessment results across multiple industries.
8. Perform a structured client handoff from AI to advisor.

## 9. Assessment Strategy

Learners receive:
- AI-generated Advisor Brief™
- CoverScore Report™
- Client profile
- CRM notes

They must:
1. Analyse the assessment.
2. Prepare for the meeting.
3. Conduct a simulated Risk Review.
4. Produce a written advisory summary.
5. Recommend next steps.

Assessment mix: practical assessment, written assessment, AI assessment, and instructor
assessment — detailed in `04-assessment/assessment-pack.md`.
