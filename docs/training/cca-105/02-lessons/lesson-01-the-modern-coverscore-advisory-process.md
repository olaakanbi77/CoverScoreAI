**Module:** CCA 105 — Practical Risk Advisory & Client Assessment
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~20 minutes | **Independent:** ~15 minutes

## 1. Lesson Overview

This first lesson reframes what a risk advisor does in the AI era. Learners discover how
the CoverScore platform automates the heavy lifting of assessment and analysis, and why
the human advisor is more essential than ever — providing judgement, trust, empathy, and
the ability to turn data into decisions. The lesson ends with a clear 8-step modern
advisory process that the entire module builds on.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Explain why the advisor role is stronger, not weaker, in an AI-powered advisory model.
2. Describe the end-to-end modern CoverScore advisory process in 8 steps.
3. Distinguish what the AI does from what the human does at each step.
4. Articulate the value proposition "AI computes, advisor advises" to a client.
5. Map where each of the remaining CCA 105 lessons fits into the process.

**The 8-Step Modern Advisory Process (slide 4 master diagram):**

1. **Assess** — Client completes the CoverScore Assessment (WhatsApp/web, CCIE-guided).
2. **Score** — Engine computes CoverScore™, risk level, exposure, resilience.
3. **Analyse** — Risk Intelligence Engine (RIE) maps products, scores opportunity.
4. **Report** — CoverScore Report™ and Risk Intelligence Report delivered to the client.
5. **Brief** — Advisor Copilot generates the one-page Advisor Brief™ for the advisor.
6. **Engage** — Advisor reviews, qualifies, and contacts the client via their preferred channel.
7. **Review** — Structured Risk Review conversation (the focus of this module).
8. **Advise & Handoff** — Recommendations, action plan, follow-up, and continuous review.

## 5. Learner Handbook

### 5.1 The shift: from products to risk

Traditional advisory began with a product catalogue. The CoverScore model begins with a
diagnostic. Instead of asking "what can I sell you?", the advisor asks "what is
actually at risk and how prepared are you?". The assessment produces a score, a risk
fingerprint, and a protection gap — the conversation then becomes about the client's
reality, not the advisor's quota.

### 5.2 What the AI covers

- **Assessment flow (CCIE):** asks the right questions, in the right order, with
  validation, across 15+ industry and personal domains (SME, Healthcare, Manufacturing,
  Schools, Churches, Transport, Income, Family, Retirement, and more).
- **Scoring:** computes the CoverScore™ (0–100) and risk band, per-pillar scores,
  exposure estimate and resilience score.
- **Risk analysis (RIE):** maps each gap to specific protection products, scores the
  commercial opportunity, pre-builds quotes, and schedules follow-ups.
- **Reporting:** generates the client-facing report and AI narrative.
- **Advisor Brief:** creates a one-page, ready-to-use briefing for the advisor.

### 5.3 What the human covers

- **Judgement:** deciding which risks deserve priority in *this client's* context.
- **Translation:** explaining what the score means in plain, human language.
- **Trust:** the client must trust the advisor before they will act.
- **Discovery:** confirming and enriching the AI's data with real conversation.
- **Recommendations:** shaping protection into an affordable, sequenced action plan.
- **Handoff:** moving the client from insight to decision, and beyond to a review cadence.

### 5.4 The division of labour at a glance

| Step | System (AI) | Advisor (Human) |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Assess | Asks questions, validates, records | Confirms engagement, sets expectations |
| Score | Computes score and bands | Reads the score critically |
| Analyse | Maps gaps to products | Judges priority in context |
| Report | Generates report + narrative | Explains it, checks understanding |
| Brief | Produces Advisor Brief™ | Adds personal context, prepares questions |
| Engage | Schedules follow-ups | Contacts, qualifies, builds rapport |
| Review | Suggests questions / objection handling | Runs the structured Risk Review |
| Handoff | Tracks next actions | Recommends, plans, and follows up |

### 5.6 Key terminology

- **CoverScore™** — 0–100 overall risk score.
- **CoverScore Assessment™** — the conversational questionnaire.
- **CoverScore Report™** — the client-facing output.
- **Advisor Brief™** — the one-page advisor-facing AI summary.
- **Risk Review** — the structured advisory meeting this module teaches.
- **Opportunity Score** — 0–100 lead potential stored in the CRM.

## 7. Case Study

**Scenario:** Mrs. Adaeze runs a fast-growing bakery chain (5 outlets, 90 staff, revenue
NGN 300M). A referral partner sends her to CoverScore. She completes the assessment on
WhatsApp during a lunch break — 7 minutes. An hour later she receives her report; the
same hour, an Advisor Brief lands in the inbox of an advisor named Tunde. Tunde calls
her the next day.

**Tasks:**
1. Identify which of the 8 steps happened before Tunde's phone call.
2. Which steps of the process did the AI perform? Which did Tunde still need to perform?
3. What would happen to the client relationship if Tunde skipped the Risk Review and
   went straight to quoting products? Explain to the class.
4. Write the opening line Tunde should use — one that shows he already understands her
   business (use the idea of "I reviewed your assessment").

## 9. AI Tutor Knowledge Base

> Knowledge entries the AI Tutor may use when answering learners' questions on this lesson.

- **KB-1.1 Modern advisory definition:** The CoverScore advisory model replaces
  product-first selling with a diagnostic-first engagement. The AI computes a
  CoverScore™ (0–100), identifies per-pillar risks, exposes protection gaps, and
  generates an Advisor Brief™. The human advisor adds judgement, empathy, trust,
  explanation, and recommendation.
- **KB-1.2 The 8-step process:** Assess, Score, Analyse, Report, Brief, Engage, Review,
  Advise & Handoff. Each step is assigned to either system (CCIE/RIE) or advisor.
- **KB-1.3 CCIE & RIE:** CCIE (CoverScore Conversational Intelligence Engine) runs the
  question flow and scoring. The Risk Intelligence Engine (RIE) runs product mapping,
  opportunity scoring, the Advisor Copilot Brief, quote pre-building, follow-up
  scheduling, and the learning loop.
- **KB-1.4 Assessment domains:** 15+ prefixes cover SME, Healthcare, Manufacturing,
  School, Church, Construction, Transport, Income, Health, Family, Young Professional,
  Retirement, Entrepreneur, Home, and Motor.
- **KB-1.5 Score bands:** Excellent (85+), Strong (70–84), Developing (55–69), Needs
  Attention (40–54), Priority Improvement (25–39), Critical Improvement (<25).

Your job is not to be replaced by AI. Your job is to be the human the AI works for. The
