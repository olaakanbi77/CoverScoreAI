## 1. Lesson Overview

Everything converges here. Learners conduct a complete, end-to-end simulated engagement:
receive an Advisor Brief and report, analyse the assessment, prepare for the Risk Review,
run the review conversation, and hand the client off to recommendations and next steps.
This is the rehearsal for the module assessment — and the first structured CoverScore
Risk Review of their career.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Orchestrate the full AI-to-advisor flow within one engagement.
2. Deliver a structured Risk Review from open to close.
3. Build and present a prioritised client action plan live.
4. Perform a clean client handoff from insight to recommendations.
5. Self-assess against the module's seven certification competencies.

## 5. Learner Handbook

### 5.1 The four-phase simulation model

| Phase | What you do | Tools |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| **Analyse** | Read the Brief + report; verify facts; build the matrix; set confidence | Report, Brief, matrix template |
| **Prepare** | 6-step prep pack: hypotheses, agenda, opening, materials | Prep template, question bank |
| **Review** | Run the 6-stage arc with the client; confirm, impact, options | Conversation guides |
| **Handoff** | Build the action plan live; agree decision + next step + follow-up | Action Plan template |

### 5.2 The seven certification competencies (self-assess

1. **Assessment interpretation** — did you read the report and matrix correctly?
2. **Report explanation** — could you explain every section in plain language?
3. **Discovery questioning** — did you confirm and deepen, not interrogate?
4. **Communication** — clear, warm, technical-when-needed?
5. **Professional judgement** — prioritisation and sequencing defensible?
6. **Risk prioritisation** — right three items, right order?
7. **Client engagement** — did the client stay with you, agree actions?

Pass standard: **80%** across the weightings in `04-assessment/assessment-pack.md`.

### 5.3 The handoff — "understand" to "decide"

The handoff is the final, clean transition:

1. **Summarise** the decision gap you uncovered: "You now know continuity is your top risk."
2. **Present** the recommended path (2–3 options/sequence).
3. **Confirm** the chosen next step and its date.
4. **Log** everything: CRM task, notes, follow-up slot.

The client should leave understanding what was decided, why, and when the advisor returns.

## 7. Case Study (The Simulation Brief)

**GreenField Agro-Processing Ltd** — agro-processing, 180 permanent + 250 seasonal
employees, revenue NGN 1.2B. CoverScore **54 (Needs Attention)**. High-priority gaps:
no business interruption cover; no key-person cover on the founder Mr. Chidi Okonkwo
(54) who personally guarantees a NGN 350M loan; no succession plan; inadequate asset
protection; no cyber security despite a digital procurement platform. Strong pillars:
farm-to-factory logistics record (80), quality systems (75). Assessment completed by the
finance manager; claims confidence is medium. The Advisor Brief suggests opening with
acknowledgement of their quality systems before discussing the personal guarantee. Time:
30 minutes. Mr. Okonkwo will attend with his finance manager.

**Tasks (full simulation):**
1. Produce the Phase 1 analysis (matrix + top-3 + confidence).
2. Produce the Phase 2 prep pack (hypotheses, agenda, opening, materials).
3. Run the review live: open, discovery/confirm, impact (risk chains), options, close.
4. Build the Client Action Plan together (decision, next step, follow-up).
5. Perform the handoff and log the CRM next actions.
6. Self-assess against all seven competencies and write where you would improve.

## 8. Knowledge Check

1. The correct phase order is:
   a. Review → Analyse → Handoff → Prepare
   b. Analyse → Prepare → Review → Handoff *(correct)*
   c. Prepare → Review → Analyse → Handoff *(incorrect)*
   d. Handoff → Analyse → Prepare → Review *(incorrect)*

2. The client handoff is best completed when:
   a. The client has been left to think alone
   b. The decision, next step, and follow-up are agreed and logged *(correct)*
   c. The premium has been quoted on the phone *(incorrect)*
   d. The assessment has been redone *(incorrect)*

3. For GreenField, which priority should lead the review?
   a. Cyber security (lowest pillar)
   b. The founder's personal guarantee exposure with no key-person cover *(correct)*
   c. Seasonal staffing *(incorrect)*
   d. Quality systems *(incorrect)*

4. The module pass standard is:
   a. 50%
   b. 70%
   c. 80% *(correct)*
   d. 100%

5. During the review, "options" stage means:
   a. Presenting 2–3 paths with trade-offs for client choice *(correct)*
   b. Reading the entire product catalogue *(incorrect)*
   c. Offering the client a discount *(incorrect)*
   d. Ending the meeting *(incorrect)*

**Answers:** 1-b, 2-b, 3-b, 4-c, 5-a.

## 10. AI Coaching Prompts

- **Prompt 8.1 (Full loop):** "Run the complete four-phase simulation with the learner as
