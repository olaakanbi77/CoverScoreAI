# CCA 106 — Student Workbook (Part 3)

**Module:** CCA 106 — Risk Advisory Practice, Business Development & Client Growth
**Use:** Complete alongside the 8 lessons; submit the marked sections for their lesson's
completion standard and the module assessment.

---

## How to use this workbook

For each lesson, complete the sections after watching the video. Store them as your
personal practice bank — they become your real-world templates the day you talk to a
client.

## Lesson 1 — Professional Advisory Communication

1. Rewrite the conventional seller's opening ("We have some excellent insurance
   products…") as a professional Risk Advisor opening for a school proprietor.
2. Translate five engine phrases (score, protection gap, priority gap, confidence,
   exposure) into calm, plain client sentences.
3. Write one fear-based sentence you have heard or used, then its professional
   replacement.

## Lesson 2 — Trust-Based Client Conversations

1. Write the five pillars of trust and one behaviour that demonstrates each.
2. Record your 45-second, sales-free opening for a concerned client.
3. For three client statements, write a "poor" response and a "professional" response
   using Listen → Clarify → Confirm → Respond.

## Lesson 3 — Understanding Client Motivations & Decision Drivers

1. Write the four discovery questions and use them in one practice conversation.
2. Analyse the same risk (property protection) for three different drivers: reputation,
   rebuild cost, compliance. Note how your language changes.
3. Draft your professional response to "Your premium is too expensive."

## Lesson 4 — The CoverScore CARE™ Conversation Framework

1. Write the four CARE™ stages and their definitions from memory.
2. Build a complete CARE™ sequence (Connect, Ask, Reframe, Enable) for Florence James.
3. Recite the memory device and, in one sentence each, name the damage of skipping a
   stage.

## Lesson 5 — Turning CoverScore Insights into Advisory Conversations

1. Write your reaction-opening question for a low CoverScore report.
2. Practice the discovery chain from "no fire insurance" to property-resilience insight.
3. Write your response to a client who reveals a personal family concern.

## Lesson 6 — Business Development Using CoverScore™

1. Draw the old and CoverScore business-development models side by side.
2. Complete the four preparation questions from an Advisor Brief.
3. Map business and personal opportunities for the SR-001 client and apply the
   one-client rule.

## Lesson 7 — Growing Long-Term Client Relationships

1. Draw the transactional loop and the advisory model.
2. Map a client through the eight-stage life cycle.
3. Build a 12-month value-contact calendar (no renewals, arrears, or product launches).
4. Write your response to a cheaper-renewal offer.

## Lesson 8 — Client Engagement Simulation

1. Prepare the SR-001 engagement: one-page plan across the seven stages.
2. Record a full 15-minute practice simulation and self-assess on the 9-category rubric.
3. Write your final response to the personal-need reveal.

## Optional extension (module assessment prep)

Re-run the Lesson 8 simulation against the module assessment rubric in
`04-assessment/assessment-pack.md` under exam conditions the day before your assessment.
