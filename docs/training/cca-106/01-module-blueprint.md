# CCA 106 — Part 1: Module Blueprint

**Module Title:** CCA 106 — Risk Advisory Practice, Business Development & Client Growth
**Package Production Standard:** Complete Training Package v2 (Parts 1–6)

---

## 1. Module Overview

CCA 106 develops the Advisor's ability to turn CoverScore intelligence into meaningful
human conversations, trusted advisory relationships, and sustainable business growth.
The Advisor is no longer simply interpreting a report — the Advisor must now:

**Communicate → Connect → Understand → Advise → Develop → Grow → Retain**

The module therefore focuses on the human capabilities that cannot simply be automated
by the CoverScore platform.

## 2. Module Purpose

Positioned between CCA 105 (interpret the intelligence) and CCA 107 (manage the
relationship), CCA 106 teaches how intelligence is converted into human advisory
conversations: professional communication, trust, motivation discovery, the CARE™
framework, legitimate opportunity identification, risk-led business development, and
long-term client relationships.

## 4. Learning Outcomes

By the end of CCA 106, the learner will be able to:

1. Communicate professionally with prospects and clients.
2. Establish trust during advisory conversations.
3. Understand client motivations and decision drivers.
4. Use the CoverScore CARE™ framework.
5. Convert CoverScore insights into meaningful conversations.
6. Develop business through risk-led conversations and legitimate advisory opportunities.
7. Grow and retain long-term client relationships.
8. Conduct a complete CoverScore client-engagement conversation.

## 5. Competencies Developed

### Professional (conversation)
- Professional advisory communication (vs conventional selling)
- Trust building and trust recovery
- Motivation and decision-driver discovery
- CARE™ framework application
- CoverScore interpretation in plain language

### Business Development
- Risk-based opportunity identification (one-client rule)
- Personal-funnel expansion without product dumping
- Referral discipline
- Value-based relationship and retention
- Annual review and relationship expansion

### Behavioural
- Client-first mindset (never product-first)
- No fear-based or pressure language
- Honesty over convenience
- Professional judgment under uncertainty
- Long-term value thinking

## 9. Assessment Strategy

**Instrument:** One 15-minute client-engagement simulation, scored on 8 parts, 100
points: Professional Communication 15, Trust & Rapport 10, Client Motivations 10, CARE™
Application 20, CoverScore Conversation 15, Business Development 10, Relationship Growth
10, Professional Judgment 10.

**Pass standard:** 80–100 Competent · 70–79 Conditional Pass · 60–69 Remediation
Required · below 60 Not Yet Competent. Any critical failure (product pitch, ignored
concerns, fear urgency, unsupported promises, irrelevant or overwhelming products,
ignored personal needs, premature close, score-as-diagnosis, misrepresented AI
intelligence, transfer-before-reduction) means not competent until coaching is completed.

**Scenario:** SR-001 (Flourish College); the assessor reveals a personal family concern
mid-conversation, which the learner must address separately and respectfully.

## 11. Certification Standard

To pass, learners must demonstrate competence through a live client-engagement
simulation across eight parts at a total of **80 / 100 or above**, with no critical
failure condition triggered. Certification is logged in the Academy with the per-part
score breakdown retained for audit.
