## Module Identity

- **Position in the Academy:** CCA 105 interprets the intelligence → **CCA 106 uses the
  intelligence** → CCA 107 manages the relationship → CCA 108 demonstrates the complete
  advisory cycle.
- **Central question:** How does an Advisor turn CoverScore intelligence into meaningful
  human conversation, trusted advisory relationships, and sustainable business growth?
- **Theme:** professional communication, trust, client motivation, the CARE™ framework,
  risk-led business development, long-term relationships, and the end-to-end client
  engagement.

## How To Use This Package

1. **Producers / course creators** — Start with `01-module-blueprint.md`, then produce
   lessons in sequence using the fixed 12-section template in each lesson file and the
   `06-multimedia` production pack.
2. **Instructors** — Use `03-resources/01-instructor-guide.md` to run trust-building
   workshops, motivation drills, and the Lesson 8 simulation, supported by
   `03-resources/05-conversation-guides.md`.
3. **Learners** — Work through Lessons 1–8 using the Learner Handbook section in each
   lesson and `03-resources/02-student-workbook.md`.
4. **AI systems** — Load the prompts in `05-ai-companion` into the AI Tutor, AI Coach,
   AI feedback and AI grading engines. The per-lesson Knowledge Base entries live inside
   each lesson file.
5. **Assessors** — Run the assessment pack in `04-assessment/assessment-pack.md`: a
   15-minute client-engagement simulation scored on the 100-point, 8-part model.

## Terminology Reference (used across this package)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| CoverScore™ | The 0–100 overall risk score from the assessment engine |
| CoverScore Report™ / Risk Story™ | The client-facing output after scoring |
| Advisor Brief™ | One-page AI-generated briefing given to the advisor |
| CARE™ | The canonical conversation framework: Connect → Ask → Reframe → Enable |
| Risk Review | The structured meeting where the advisor walks the client through the report |
| One-Client Rule | One client ≠ one policy, and one client ≠ every policy — maximum relevance |
| Personal Funnel™ | Personal assessment categories (Income Protection, Retirement Readiness, etc.) |
| Opportunity Score | 0–100 lead potential stored in the CRM |
| CCIE | CoverScore Conversational Intelligence Engine (question flow & scoring) |

Score bands used in the platform (canonical, per the Intelligence Standards™):
**Highly Resilient** (80–100), **Moderately Resilient** (60–79), **Needs Improvement**
(40–59), **High Risk** (20–39), **Critical Risk** (0–19).

Advisory workflow (canonical): the **10-step workflow** — Receive, Review, Prepare,
Discover, Interpret, Prioritise, Advise, Agree, Document, Follow Through. Risk
Reduction™ is always presented before Risk Transfer™, and the two are never mixed.

## Certification Standard (recap)

The certification instrument is one **15-minute client-engagement simulation** scored on
8 parts (Professional Communication 15, Trust & Rapport 10, Client Motivations 10, CARE™
Application 20, CoverScore Conversation 15, Business Development 10, Relationship Growth
10, Professional Judgment 10). Pass bands: 80–100 Competent · 70–79 Conditional Pass ·
60–69 Remediation Required · below 60 Not Yet Competent. Full detail in
`04-assessment/assessment-pack.md`.
