# CCA 106 — Lesson 4: The CoverScore CARE™ Conversation Framework

**Module:** CCA 106 — Risk Advisory Practice, Business Development & Client Growth
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~25 minutes | **Independent:** ~15 minutes

## 1. Lesson Overview

This lesson introduces CARE™, the core human conversation framework of the CoverScore
advisory model. Learners master four stages — Connect, Ask, Reframe, Enable — and learn
why every advisory conversation must pass through all of them in order. This framework
is canonical across the Academy and is assessed directly in the CCA 106 module assessment.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. State the four CARE™ stages: Connect, Ask, Reframe, Enable.
2. Explain the purpose of each stage and why the order cannot be skipped.
3. Open a conversation (Connect) with rapport and context.
4. Explore the client's world (Ask) with genuine curiosity.
5. Connect client concerns to risk intelligence (Reframe) honestly.
6. Move the client toward action (Enable) with clear next steps.

## 5. Learner Handbook

### 5.1 The CARE™ framework

- **Connect** — establish rapport and context. Thank the client, confirm purpose, and
  open with curiosity. Connect always precedes advice.
- **Ask** — explore the client's concerns, priorities and experiences with genuine
  curiosity. Ask before you assume.
- **Reframe** — connect what the client told you to the risk intelligence. Their concern
  meets the report honestly, including weaknesses the report identifies.
- **Enable** — help the client understand practical next steps and move toward action.
  Identify the most important actions first; then decide where financial protection
  supports remaining exposure.

### 5.2 Why the order matters

CARE™ is sequential: C before A, A before R, R before E. Never skip a stage. Asking the
client before advising, and reframing their concern before recommending, is what keeps
the conversation client-led. Skipping straight to "Enable" without understanding
produces product-first selling.

### 5.3 The memory device

> "Connect before you advise.
> Ask before you assume.
> Reframe before you recommend.
> Enable before you close."

### 5.4 CARE™ and Risk Reduction / Risk Transfer

Reframe is where risk intelligence is presented — always Risk Reduction™ first, Risk
Transfer™ second, never mixed. Enable turns that into a sequenced, agreed action plan
with clear next steps.

### 5.5 CARE™ anchors in the module

Lesson 1's five communication principles support every CARE stage. Lesson 2's trust
pillars and listening sequence give Connect and Ask their depth. Lesson 3's discovery
questions feed Ask. Lessons 5–8 apply CARE™ to reports, business development,
relationships, and the simulation.

## 7. Case Study

**Scenario:** In a Risk Review, Florence James says, "I was mainly concerned about
student accidents." The report (SR-001) shows weak emergency procedures, an untested
alarm, unserviced extinguishers, and a liability gap.

**Tasks:**
1. Write your Connect line (Scene 1).
2. Write the Ask line that follows her concern truthfully (Scene 2).
3. Write your Reframe line connecting her concern to the report's findings (Scene 3).
4. Write your Enable line: immediate actions first, financial protection for remaining
   exposure (Scene 4).
5. Mark each line C/A/R/E and check no stage is skipped.

## 9. AI Tutor Knowledge Base

- **KB-4.1 CARE™ definition (canonical):** Connect (rapport and context), Ask (explore
  concerns, priorities, experiences), Reframe (connect concerns to risk intelligence),
  Enable (practical next steps and action). The canonical framework for every client
  conversation across CCA 105–108.
- **KB-4.2 CARE™ rules:** C before A, A before R, R before E. Never skip a stage. Ask is
  driven by genuine curiosity. Reframe is always reduction-first. Enable ends with agreed
  next steps.
- **KB-4.3 Connect:** Establish rapport and context; open with curiosity — e.g., "What
  prompted you to complete the assessment in the first place?"
- **KB-4.4 Ask:** Explore the client's world with curiosity — e.g., "Have there been any
  incidents recently that influenced that concern?"
- **KB-4.5 Reframe:** Connect the client's concern to the risk intelligence — e.g.,
  "Your concern is consistent with the report, which also shows weaknesses in emergency
  procedures and liability protection."
- **KB-4.6 Enable:** Sequence actions — most important first — then place financial
  protection where exposure remains. End with agreed next steps, dates, and ownership.
