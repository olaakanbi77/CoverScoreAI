# CCA 106 — Lesson 5: Turning CoverScore Insights into Advisory Conversations

**Module:** CCA 106 — Risk Advisory Practice, Business Development & Client Growth
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~25 minutes | **Independent:** ~15 minutes

---

## 1. Lesson Overview

This lesson teaches how AI-generated intelligence becomes human advisory dialogue. The
report is the starting point, not the conversation. Learners practise turning report
findings into discovery, interpreting patterns in the client's real-world context, and
extending the conversation to legitimate personal advisory opportunities — without
product dumping.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Explain why "the report is not the conversation — it is the starting point."
2. Open a report-review conversation with the client's reaction, not the numbers.
3. Deepen discovery from a stated gap to the underlying operational reality.
4. Convert an insight into a legitimate advisory opportunity.
5. Handle a revealed personal concern without mixing personal and business decisions.

## 5. Learner Handbook

### 5.1 The report is a starting point

The report organises intelligence; it does not make decisions. The advisor turns its
findings into dialogue. Leading with the client's reaction — "What was your reaction
when you saw that?" — turns a briefing into a conversation.

### 5.2 From findings to the client's operation

A finding such as "no fire insurance" opens a door. Professional discovery walks through
it: what protection exists, when was it last inspected, who maintains it. Uncertainty in
the client's answers (e.g., "I'm not sure" about inspections) is itself valuable
evidence of a broader property-resilience problem — not merely an insurance gap.

### 5.3 Human interpretation

AI identifies patterns across the data. The Advisor decides what the pattern means in
this client's real-world context: the building, the operation, the consequences, the
priorities. That contextual judgement is the human contribution.

### 5.4 Insight to opportunity

Insight (property weakness) leads to conversation (facility exposure), then discovery
(building value, controls, interruption risk), then a legitimate advisory opportunity
(property + liability + business continuity review). The opportunity is earned by the
conversation, not assumed from the report.

### 5.5 The personal funnel: expand, don't dump

If the client reveals a personal concern (family protection), acknowledge it and offer a
*separate* conversation. Mixing personal and business decisions in one pitch is product
dumping. The Advisor only introduces a personal solution when the conversation reveals a
relevant need — never merely because it exists.

## 6. Practical Exercises

**Exercise 5.1 — Report to conversation (15 min)**
Using the SR-001 findings (no fire insurance, weak alarm testing, rare maintenance,
liability gap), write a full opening sequence: reaction question, follow-up on current
protection, and one deeper question per finding.

**Exercise 5.2 — Interpret the pattern (15 min)**
The client's extinguishers were last inspected "not sure." Write the professional
response that reframes this as a broader property-resilience issue without fear-selling.

**Exercise 5.3 — The separate conversation (15 min)**
Florence reveals she is worried about her family's future. Write the Script Scene 7
response and explain why you keep personal and business decisions separate.

**Exercise 5.4 — Opportunity map (15 min)**
Build the Insight → Opportunity map for one SR-001 finding: insight, conversation,
discovery, opportunity.

## 9. AI Tutor Knowledge Base

- **KB-5.1 Report as starting point:** The report organises intelligence; the advisor
  creates the conversation. Open report reviews with the client's reaction.
- **KB-5.2 Discovery chain:** Follow each finding into the client's operation (current
  protection, inspections, maintenance). Client uncertainty is evidence of a broader
  resilience issue.
- **KB-5.3 Human interpretation:** Advisors give patterns meaning in the client's
  real-world context — building, operation, consequences, priorities.
- **KB-5.4 Insight → opportunity:** Insight → conversation → discovery → legitimate
  advisory opportunity. Opportunity is earned by the conversation.
- **KB-5.5 Personal funnel rule:** Address revealed personal concerns in a separate
  conversation; never mix personal and business decisions; never introduce a solution
  that the conversation has not revealed as relevant.
