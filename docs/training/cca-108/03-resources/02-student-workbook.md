# CCA 108 — Student Workbook (Part 3)

**Module:** CCA 108 — Capstone: Integrated Advisory Simulation & Professional Assessment
**Use:** Complete alongside the 8 lessons; submit the marked sections for their lesson's
completion standard and the module assessment.

---

## How to use this workbook

For each lesson, complete the sections after watching the video. These become your
personal practice bank — the same material you will use to operate the final case
independently.

## Lesson 1 — Capstone Orientation & the Complete CoverScore Advisory Cycle

1. Write the Capstone principle and the Capstone rule from memory.
2. Draw the complete CoverScore Operating Model, marking what AI does and what the
   Advisor does at each step.
3. List the eight tested capabilities and, for each, one action that proves it.

## Lesson 2 — Integrated Client Engagement Simulation

1. Prepare the Flourish College case with the three-column method: What do I know? What
   don't I know? What must I discover?
2. Write your opening line (no conclusions before the client speaks) — compare with the
   gold standard.
3. Write five discovery questions for "Student safety worries me because we've had an
   accident before."

## Lesson 3 — Complex Risk Intelligence & CoverScore Assessment

1. Sort the new information into positive / negative / new / incident.
2. Explain the 44 trap: what the headline says vs what the pillars say (Transport 43→38).
3. Produce the six-task list and assign an Avoid / Reduce / Transfer / Accept response
   to each major exposure.

## Lesson 4 — Protection Strategy & Executive Advisory Simulation

1. Write the four responses (Reduce, Transfer, Accept, Avoid) with one example each.
2. Build the strategy map across student safety, property, transport, and continuity.
3. Convert the weak product list into the strong two-layer executive message.
4. List the six decision items and the next meeting.

## Lesson 5 — Business Development, Conversion & Client Growth Simulation

1. Build the opportunity map and mark which items are conversation-supported.
2. Write your response to the personal reveal (separate conversation, no pitch).
3. Write your "already spending too much" objection response and your referral line.

## Lesson 6 — Professional Practice, Portfolio Management & Risk Review Simulation

1. Complete the ten-input preparation checklist for the one-year-later review.
2. Write your claim-review sequence for the student injury (acknowledge → handle →
   understand → lessons → controls → residual exposure).
3. Produce the six-field CRM record (Situation, Intelligence, Discussion, Advice,
   Decision, Next Step).

## Lesson 7 — Ethics, Professional Judgment & Difficult Advisory Decisions

1. Write the gold-standard inflated-value refusal and the coverage-pressure response.
2. Run the X → Y human-oversight sequence on the Cyber Risk recommendation.
3. For six dilemmas, choose proceed / pause / clarify / escalate / decline / obtain
   information and justify.

## Lesson 8 — Final Integrated Advisory Simulation & Professional Assessment

1. Prepare the Darman Schools case (27 → 56) across Phase 1: known, unresolved, emerging,
   priorities, opportunities, gaps.
2. Write the full fifteen-phase outline with timings.
3. Write your responses to the six objections, the second-campus change, the injury with
   litigation threat, and the change-the-information pressure.
4. Produce the final advisory record and the client handoff.
5. Record a full practice simulation and self-assess on the 10-part scorecard.

## Optional extension (module assessment prep)

`04-assessment/assessment-pack.md` under exam conditions the day before your assessment.
During the final simulation, expect the evaluator to inject a change, an incident, and
