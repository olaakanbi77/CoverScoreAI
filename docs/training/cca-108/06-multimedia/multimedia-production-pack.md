(colours, type, contrast, one-idea-per-slide rule). Diagrams reused across the programme

lock (canonical): Highly Resilient = green; Moderately Resilient = light green/teal;
Needs Improvement = amber; High Risk = deep red; Critical Risk = darkest red.

| A6 | Four-response grid | Reduce/Transfer/Accept/Avoid cards slot into a quadrant |
| A7 | Weak vs strong | Product list shrinks; two-layer strategy grows |
| A8 | Fifteen-phase bar | Phases light 1–15 in order; never skipped or scrabbled |

| A10 | Academy pathway close | Full pathway builds Knowledge→Mindset→Assessment→Advisory→Growth→Portfolio→Capstone |

**Motion rules:** 300–500 ms transitions; no flashing; honour reduced-motion; urgency
cues never strobe (ethics + accessibility).
