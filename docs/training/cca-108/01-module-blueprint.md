# CCA 108 — Part 1: Module Blueprint

**Module Title:** CCA 108 — Capstone: Integrated Advisory Simulation & Professional Assessment
**Package Production Standard:** Complete Training Package v2 (Parts 1–6)

---

## 1. Module Overview

CCA 108 is the final professional competency module of the CoverScore Certified Advisor
pathway. Unlike previous modules, CCA 108 is not primarily about learning another body of
knowledge — it is about **demonstrating professional capability**.

The Advisor must prove that they can take a real or simulated client from Discovery,
through Assessment, Risk Intelligence, Human Interpretation, Advisory Conversation,
Strategy, Business Development, and Portfolio Management, and ultimately into a
sustainable long-term relationship.

**Capstone principle:**

> *Knowledge is not competence. Competence is the ability to apply knowledge correctly in
> a real situation.*

**Capstone rule:**

> *Do not sell because you found a product. Advise because you found a need.*

## 2. Module Purpose

Positioned as the final stage of the Academy, CCA 108 integrates everything from CCA
101–107. Previous modules asked "Do you understand?"; CCA 108 asks "Can you perform?".
The module certifies the learner's ability to operate the complete CoverScore advisory
cycle independently and ethically.

## 4. Learning Outcomes

By the end of CCA 108, the learner will be able to:

1. Manage an end-to-end CoverScore advisory engagement.
2. Interpret assessment intelligence, including complex and conflicting signals.
3. Analyse complex risk situations.
4. Conduct professional discovery.
5. Apply the CARE™ conversation framework.
6. Distinguish operational risk reduction from risk transfer.
7. Develop appropriate protection strategies.
8. Communicate recommendations to decision-makers.
9. Identify legitimate business-development opportunities.
10. Manage objections and difficult decisions.
11. Maintain professional judgment under uncertainty.
12. Manage client portfolio considerations.
13. Conduct post-loss advisory conversations.
14. Document advisory decisions.
15. Demonstrate ethical professional behaviour.
16. Create a clear long-term client-management strategy.

## 5. Competencies Developed

### Technical
- Complex risk-intelligence interpretation (beyond the headline score)
- CoverScore assessment, pillar, and signal analysis
- Protection strategy development (Reduce / Transfer / Accept / Avoid)
- Portfolio alignment and risk-review delivery
- Risk documentation and CRM records

### Professional
- Executive advisory communication
- Business development with the relevance rule
- Claim and post-loss advisory
- Professional judgment under uncertainty
- Ethical decision-making under commercial pressure

### Behavioural
- Client-first, need-first identity (never product-first)
- Integrity: refusal, confidentiality, escalation
- Human oversight of AI (AI as source, not decision-maker)
- Long-term relationship management
- Independent professional capability

## 9. Assessment Strategy

**Instrument:** One Final Integrated Advisory Simulation — Darman Schools Group — scored
on 10 parts, 100 points: Risk Intelligence 10, Professional Communication 10, Discovery &
Client Understanding 10, CARE™ Framework 10, Professional Judgment 15, Advisory Strategy
15, Business Development 10, Portfolio Management 10, Ethics & Integrity 5, Documentation
& Handoff 5.

**Pass standard:** 85–100 Certified Advisor Ready · 75–84 Competent · 65–74 Conditional
Pass · below 65 Not Yet Competent. Any critical fail condition (misrepresentation,
misleading advice, unsuitable recommendation for gain, fabricated information, ignored
material risk, unsupported guarantees, AI treated as unquestionable, confidentiality
breach, pressure tactics, failure to escalate) bars certification regardless of total.

**Scenario:** Darman Schools Group (Lagos, Emmanuel Dairo, CoverScore 27 → 56) across the
15-phase cycle, with evaluator-injected changes, incidents, and ethical pressure.

## 11. Certification Standard

To pass, learners must demonstrate independent professional capability through the Final
Integrated Advisory Simulation at a total of **85 / 100 or above**, with no critical fail
condition triggered. Certification is logged in the Academy with the per-part score
breakdown retained for audit. The final certification decision is Certified, Conditional,
or Not Certified.
