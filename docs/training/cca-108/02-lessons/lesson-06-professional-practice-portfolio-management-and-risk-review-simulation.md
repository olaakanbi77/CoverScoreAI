# CCA 108 — Lesson 6: Professional Practice, Portfolio Management & Risk Review Simulation

**Module:** CCA 108 — Capstone: Integrated Advisory Simulation & Professional Assessment
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~40 minutes (simulation) | **Independent:** ~15 minutes

---

## 1. Lesson Overview

This lesson tests whether the learner can manage the client after the initial engagement.
One year later, Flourish College has changed: a second campus, a larger population, new
buses, new staff, improved controls, one claim, and new laboratory equipment. The learner
must review every changed dimension, conduct the client review ("What has changed since
our last review?"), handle the claim properly, align the portfolio, produce a six-field
CRM record, and demonstrate that they are managing risk, protection, relationship, and
future opportunity.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Prepare a one-year-later review from previous and current CoverScore, previous
   recommendations, implementation status, claims, incidents, new assets, new activities,
   existing protection, and CRM history.
2. Open the client review with change, not renewal.
3. Handle a reported claim professionally: acknowledge, ensure handling, understand,
   lessons, controls, residual exposure.
4. Test portfolio alignment: property, vehicles, liability, laboratory, staff.
5. Produce a professional CRM record (Situation, Intelligence, Discussion, Advice, Decision, Next Step).
6. Demonstrate relationship health across Risk, Protection, Relationship, and Future Opportunity.

## 8. Knowledge Check

1. The professional client-review opening is:
   a. "Are you ready for renewal?" *(incorrect)*
   b. "What has changed since our last review?" *(correct)*
   c. "Would you like a discount?" *(incorrect)*
   d. "Do you have a claim to file?" *(incorrect)*

2. On hearing of the student injury claim, the FIRST thing the Advisor does is:
   a. Check the policy limit *(incorrect)*
   b. Acknowledge the incident (human-first) *(correct)*
   c. Sell more liability cover *(incorrect)*
   d. Read the exclusion clause *(incorrect)*

3. Portfolio alignment requires checking whether:
   a. Property protection reflects the second campus *(correct)*
   b. The premium is unchanged *(incorrect)*
   c. The policy name is correct *(incorrect)*
   d. Any discount applies *(incorrect)*

4. The CRM record's six fields are:
   a. Situation, Intelligence, Discussion, Advice, Decision, Next Step *(correct)*
   b. Name, Address, Phone, Email, Policy, Premium *(incorrect)*
   c. Score, Pillar, Gap, Fix, Sale, Close *(incorrect)*
   d. Client, Product, Price, Date, Agent, Renewal *(incorrect)*

5. Relationship health means managing:
   a. Risk × Protection × Relationship × Future Opportunity *(correct)*
   b. Only new sales *(incorrect)*
   c. Only claims *(incorrect)*
   d. Only the premium *(incorrect)*

**Answers:** 1-b, 2-b, 3-a, 4-a, 5-a.
