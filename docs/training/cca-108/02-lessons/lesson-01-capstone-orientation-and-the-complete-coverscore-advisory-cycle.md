# CCA 108 — Lesson 1: Capstone Orientation & the Complete CoverScore Advisory Cycle

**Module:** CCA 108 — Capstone: Integrated Advisory Simulation & Professional Assessment
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~25 minutes | **Independent:** ~15 minutes

---

## 1. Lesson Overview

CCA 108 is the final professional competency module of the CoverScore Certified Advisor
pathway. Unlike previous modules, it is not primarily about learning another body of
knowledge — it is about **demonstrating professional capability**. This opening lesson
establishes the standard expected of a Certified CoverScore Advisor, introduces the
complete CoverScore Operating Model, and sets the Capstone principle that governs
everything that follows: knowledge is not competence; competence is the ability to apply
knowledge correctly in a real situation.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Explain the purpose and position of CCA 108 within the Academy pathway.
2. State the Capstone principle and the shift from "Do you understand?" to "Can you perform?".
3. Track a client through the complete CoverScore Operating Model.
4. Name the eight capabilities the Capstone tests.
5. Define the human value the Advisor provides beyond AI-generated intelligence.
6. Apply the Capstone rule: advise because you found a need, not because you found a product.

## 5. Learner Handbook

### 5.1 Module positioning

CCA 108 is the final professional competency module of the pathway. It is not another
body of knowledge — it is the demonstration of professional capability. The Advisor must
prove they can take a real or simulated client from Discovery through Assessment, Risk
Intelligence, Human Interpretation, Advisory Conversation, Strategy, Business
Development, Portfolio Management, and into a sustainable long-term relationship.

### 5.2 The Capstone principle

> **Knowledge is not competence.**
>
> **Competence is the ability to apply knowledge correctly in a real situation.**

Previous modules asked "Do you understand?"; CCA 108 asks "Can you perform?"

### 5.3 The Advisor journey

- **CCA 101–104** — Knowledge + Mindset
- **CCA 105** — Assessment
- **CCA 106** — Advisory + Growth
- **CCA 107** — Portfolio Management
- **CCA 108** — Professional Integration

### 5.4 What the Capstone tests

Eight capabilities: Risk Intelligence, Professional Judgment, Communication, Client
Empathy, Advisory Strategy, Business Development, Portfolio Management, Ethics.

### 5.5 The human value

CoverScore automates much of the information process. The Advisor's role is not to
compete with the AI — it is to create the human value the AI cannot independently
provide: interpret, question, understand, exercise judgment, advise, build trust, and
take responsibility for the quality of the professional relationship.

### 5.6 The complete CoverScore Operating Model

AI discovers → AI assesses → AI generates intelligence → Advisor interprets → Advisor
advises → Advisor builds strategy → AI automates follow-up → Advisor manages long-term
relationship → AI reassessment → Advisor continuously improves the relationship.

### 5.7 The Capstone rule

> **Do not sell because you found a product. Advise because you found a need.**

The Capstone is not a product-selling test. It is a professional advisory test.

## 6. Practical Exercises

**Exercise 1.1 — The standard (15 min)**
Restate the Capstone principle in your own words and write what "Can you perform?"
means for a Risky Review, an objection, a difficult decision, and a post-loss call.

**Exercise 1.2 — The operating model (15 min)**
Draw the complete CoverScore Operating Model from memory, with one thing the Advisor
does at each human stage.

**Exercise 1.3 — Sell vs advise (15 min)**
For each of six client situations, decide whether the professional response is sell
(product-found) or advise (need-found), and write the advising line.

## 7. Case Study

**Scenario:** You are about to manage the final case — Darman Schools Group (Lagos,
Emmanuel Dairo). You must operate the complete advisory cycle.

**Tasks:**
1. Map the case through the Operating Model, naming what AI does versus what you do.
2. Write the Capstone rule applied to the scenario (need before product).
3. State the eight capabilities you will be demonstrating and how each will appear.
