# CCA 108 — Lesson 2: Integrated Client Engagement Simulation

**Module:** CCA 108 — Capstone: Integrated Advisory Simulation & Professional Assessment
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~60 minutes (full simulation) | **Independent:** ~20 minutes

---

## 1. Lesson Overview

This lesson begins the first complete client simulation. Using the module's opening case —
Flourish College (Jos, Florence James, Proprietress, CoverScore 18/100, Critical) — the
learner must prepare for the engagement, conduct the opening, complete discovery, reframe
client concern against assessment intelligence, enable a clear next step, and be scored
on opening, listening, questioning, interpretation, professionalism, and judgment. The
simulation tests whether the learner can turn intelligence into a real advisory
conversation.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Prepare for a first client engagement from the CoverScore assessment, Advisor Brief™,
   Risk Report™, client history, and CRM information.
2. Run the opening conversation (gold-standard line) without drawing conclusions before
   the client speaks.
3. Explore the client's revealed concern (student safety after a past accident).
4. Reframe client concern × assessment intelligence → relevant risk discussion.
5. Enable immediate priorities, operational actions, protection considerations, further
   information required, and next steps.
6. Self-assess against opening, listening, questioning, interpretation, professionalism,
   and judgment.

## 5. Learner Handbook

### 5.1 The simulation case

The first capstone simulation reuses Flourish College (SR-001): Jos, Florence James,
Proprietress. Initial CoverScore 18/100 — Critical. Pillars: Transport Safety 43,
Business Continuity 35, Student Safety 32, Regulatory Readiness 30, Property Protection 5.
Initial findings include accident history, no emergency procedures, uninspected fire
extinguishers, no working fire alarm, rare maintenance, no Public Liability, no
comprehensive Fire Insurance, weak business continuity, operated buses, and untrained
drivers.

### 5.2 Preparation (Scene 1)

The learner receives the CoverScore Assessment, Advisor Brief™, Risk Report™, client
history, and CRM information. Preparation is scored: what do I know (assessment
intelligence), what don't I know (client context), what must I discover (priorities,
motivations, circumstances, decision drivers).

### 5.3 The opening (Scene 2)

Open with curiosity, not conclusions. The gold-standard line thanks the client, states
the report was reviewed, defers conclusions until the client speaks, and asks for the
main concern behind the assessment.

### 5.4 Discovery (Scene 3)

When the client reveals "Student safety worries me because we've had an accident
before," the Advisor must explore — what happened, what changed, what is managed now,
what worries her most.

### 5.5 Reframe (Scene 4)

Connect the client's concern to the assessment intelligence so the discussion is
relevant: the concern is a real finding, and the intelligence gives it structure.

### 5.6 Enable (Scene 5)

Close the enable stage with immediate priorities, operational actions, protection
considerations to explore, further information required, and a clear next step.

### 5.7 Evaluation (Scene 6)

The evaluator scores opening, listening, questioning, interpretation, professionalism,
and judgment.

## 6. Practical Exercises

**Exercise 2.1 — Prepare the case (20 min)**
From the case card, fill three columns: What do I know? What don't I know? What must I
discover? Include at least three discovery questions.

**Exercise 2.2 — Open with curiosity (15 min)**
Write and practise the opening line. Your version must not draw a conclusion before the
client speaks.

**Exercise 2.3 — Explore the concern (15 min)**
Plan five follow-up questions for "Student safety worries me because we've had an
accident before."

**Exercise 2.4 — Peer simulation (30 min)**
Run the opening, discovery, and reframe with a partner; score each other on the six
evaluation criteria.

## 7. Case Study

**Scenario:** You receive the Flourish College pack (CoverScore 18, Critical). Your job is
the first engagement with Florence James.

**Tasks:**
1. Write your three-column preparation (know / don't know / must discover).
2. Write your opening line and explain why it defers conclusions.
3. Write five discovery questions for the student-safety concern.
4. Write your Reframe line connecting her concern to Property Protection and Student
   Safety findings.
5. Write your Enable close: immediate priority, one operational action, one protection
   consideration, and the next step you would agree.
