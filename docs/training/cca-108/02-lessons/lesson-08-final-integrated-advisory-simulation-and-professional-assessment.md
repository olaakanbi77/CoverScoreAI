# CCA 108 — Lesson 8: Final Integrated Advisory Simulation & Professional Assessment

**Module:** CCA 108 — Capstone: Integrated Advisory Simulation & Professional Assessment
**Video duration:** ~45 minutes | **Workshop:** ~120 minutes (full simulation) | **Independent:** ~30 minutes

## 1. Lesson Overview

This lesson evaluates the learner's complete ability to function as a CoverScore Certified
Advisor. The learner receives a complete client case — Darman Schools Group, Lagos, Owner
Emmanuel Dairo — containing incomplete information, conflicting signals, multiple
stakeholders, commercial opportunity, operational risk, personal risk, previous
recommendations, previous decisions, incident history, existing insurance, emerging risks,
and potential objections. The learner must independently conduct the entire advisory
cycle across fifteen phases, be scored on the ten-part, 100-point scorecard, and avoid
every critical fail condition. The lesson closes with the final Capstone debrief and the
Academy's final outcome.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:

1. Prepare for the final case from all available intelligence.
2. Conduct the complete fifteen-phase advisory cycle independently.
3. Interpret complex, incomplete, and conflicting intelligence.
4. Distinguish operational risk reduction from risk transfer.
5. Identify legitimate business opportunities without product dumping.
6. Handle objections and difficult decisions professionally.
7. Manage portfolio change and post-loss scenarios.
8. Demonstrate professional integrity under ethical pressure.
9. Produce the final advisory record and client handoff.
10. Self-assess against the ten-part, 100-point scorecard.

9. **Handle Objections** — the evaluator's objection list.
10. **Conversion** — a realistic next action.

12. **Post-Loss Scenario** — an injury with a litigation threat.
